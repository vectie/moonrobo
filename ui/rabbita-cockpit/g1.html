About this run.

What counts as a failure?

A root height deviation above 0.25 m or an upright-axis deviation above 0.8 ends a rollout. The return denominator still uses the planned full duration.

Which motions were tested?

Both checkpoints started at frame zero on the same 40 preselected motions from the BONES-SEED training catalog.

Which model is running here?

The default policy and world model are MoonRobo’s iteration-14,000 BF16 exports. Switching to iteration 4 replaces the policy; the learned dynamics stay at iteration 14,000.

Why can the live result differ?

MuJoCo WASM and native APIC use different physics implementations. Browser arithmetic and pushes can also change the trajectory.

Has this policy run on a physical robot?

We evaluated the 29-joint G1 in simulation. Physical G1 and the 24-motor Noetix E1 have not been validated with this checkpoint.
